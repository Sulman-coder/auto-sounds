Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains the terms that apply to payments and refund requests related to AutoSound Guide services.

General Policy

AutoSound Guide aims to provide helpful and reliable car audio information, guides and support. Because our services and resources may involve time, digital information or personalized assistance, refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis.

Eligibility for a Refund

A refund may be considered when a customer has been charged incorrectly, has experienced a duplicate payment, or when a paid service cannot be delivered as described.

  • Duplicate or accidental charges may be reviewed for a refund.
  • Refund requests should be submitted as soon as possible.
  • Requests are reviewed based on the circumstances of the payment.

Non-Refundable Services

Digital information, completed support work, personalized assistance or services that have already been provided may not be eligible for a refund. Refund eligibility may vary depending on the nature of the service.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us through the Contact Us page and provide the details of your payment and the reason for your request. We may ask for additional information to verify the transaction.

Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will generally be returned using the original payment method. The time required for the refund to appear in your account may depend on your payment provider or financial institution.

Changes to This Policy

AutoSound Guide may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated policy information.

Contact

If you have questions about this Refund Policy or would like to submit a refund request, please contact us through the Contact Us page.
